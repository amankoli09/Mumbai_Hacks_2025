## 💥 The Problem
In today's digital age, misinformation spreads **6x faster than truth**. A fake claim can reach millions in minutes—long before fact-checkers can respond. Whether it's deepfake videos, manipulated election news, or health misinformation during a crisis, the damage is done before verification arrives.  
The truth gap is real. And it's dangerous.

---

## ⚡ Our Solution
TruthShield is an AI-powered verification platform that **closes this gap instantly**. Paste any YouTube link, tweet, Instagram reel, or suspicious claim. Within seconds, our AI:

- Cross-references trusted fact-checkers  
- Analyzes content authenticity  
- Detects AI-generated media  
- Delivers a credibility score with transparent sourcing  

Truth now moves as fast as lies.

## 🚀 What Makes Us Unique

### 🌐 WhatsApp Forward Analyzer (India's #1 Misinformation Channel)
- Upload forwarded messages, images, or audio from WhatsApp  
- Most misinformation in India spreads through WhatsApp, not social media  
- First to tackle this massive blind spot  

### 🗣️ Regional Language Support
- Verify content in multiple Indian languages (Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati & more)  
- Bridges the gap left by English-only fact-checkers  

### 🎭 Deepfake & AI-Generated Content Detection
- Identify manipulated videos, AI-generated images, and synthetic audio  
- Visual heatmaps showing tampered regions  

### 🔄 Viral Prediction Engine
- Predict what will go viral before it spreads  
- Real-time monitoring across platforms  

### 🧩 Misinformation Family Tree
- Track how fake claims mutate and spread  
- Visualize origin, evolution, and geographical spread  

### ⚡ Browser Extension with Inline Warnings
- Real-time flags appear automatically as you scroll  
- Works on Twitter, Facebook, YouTube, Instagram
